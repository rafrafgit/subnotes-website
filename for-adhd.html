Common questions

Do I have to set due dates for everything?

No. Tasks can sit in Next, Soon or Someday with no date at all, and Today only shows what is actually due today or overdue — so nothing turns red for being ignored.

Does Subnotes remind me about tasks?

On iPhone and iPad, a task with a specific time gets a notification at that time. Every platform shows a badge with the number of things due today, and the widget lists them on your Home Screen.

What makes Subnotes suit the way I think?

There is nowhere to file an idea before you have it. You write in plain text, and a line becomes a task when it turns out to be one — so capturing never waits on deciding where something belongs.
