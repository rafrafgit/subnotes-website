Subnotes for Claude Code

In short

A plugin that lets Claude Code read the notebooks you choose to connect, and add tasks to them — so “let's put that on the backlog” lands in your notebook instead of dying with the conversation. It isn't available yet.

What it will do

Ask what's on a notebook, or what to pick up next. Say “put that on the backlog” mid-conversation and it goes to the right notebook and the right section, without breaking your train of thought.

Two rules it keeps

  • It only ever adds. Nothing can be edited, deleted, completed, reordered or renamed. A write that only adds cannot lose work.
  • Only notebooks you connect are visible. Everything else is never written out at all, so it can't be read — invisible, not merely hidden.

What you'll need

Subnotes 1.4 or later, on a Mac, and Claude Code. Connecting a notebook is a right-click away: choose Connect to Claude Code. If you've already done that, nothing is broken — the other half just isn't out yet.
